Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers on charges, free days and using the calculators.

Quick answers to the questions we receive most often about the calculators, the charges and how to use the results.

About the calculators

Is the calculator free?

Yes. All calculators and guides are free to use, and no registration is needed.

Which operators are covered?

Gerry'sDnata (main calculator), SAPS (Shaheen and Thai airlines) and PIA. A Menzies calculator is planned and shown as coming soon.

How accurate are the results?

The logic follows the tariff sheets tier by tier, including free days, day brackets, weight bands, fixed charges and 15% sales tax. They are still estimates; confirm with your operator before paying.

Where do the rates come from?

From the published tariff sheets of each operator. If a tariff is revised, the rates here are updated manually, so there can be a delay.

Do you store my shipment details?

No. Calculations run in your browser and the figures you enter are not sent to our servers.

About the charges

What is godown rent?

The daily fee charged by the warehouse operator for storing your cargo. See our Godown Rent Guide.

What is demurrage?

The storage charge that grows as cargo stays longer. See Demurrage Explained.

What are D/O fee and documentation charges?

Fixed fees for the delivery order and paperwork. See Import Clearance Charges.

Why do dangerous goods and cold cargo cost more?

They need segregated or temperature-controlled space, trained handling and carry no free days. See the DG and cold storage guides.

Why is sales tax added?

The operator's service bill carries a 15% sales tax in our tariff logic, applied to the subtotal.

Using the site

How do I count days?

Arrival counts as day 1 and the gate-out date is the last day counted. Weekends and holidays count.

Why is handling "not published" for some categories?

For detained and radioactive cargo the source tariff does not publish handling charges, so the calculator marks them as to be confirmed instead of guessing.

Can I use the calculator on my phone?

Yes. The pages are built to work on mobile and desktop.

How can I report an error or suggest a feature?

Use the contact page. Please include the cargo type, weight, dates and the figure you expected.

Is this site affiliated with any airline or airport authority?

No. It is an independent reference tool.
