Documents Needed to Clear Air Cargo: A Practical Checklist

What each document does, who provides it and the mistakes that hold shipments up.

Last updated: 11 November 2026 · General information for planning; procedures and fees can change, so confirm with your clearing agent and operator.

Most clearance delays begin with paperwork, not with the cargo. A document that is missing, inconsistent or unclear can pause the process for days, and each day in the godown can cost money. This checklist explains the usual set of documents and what each one is for.

The core documents

DocumentWho issues itWhy it is needed
Commercial invoiceSupplier / exporterDeclares the value, description and terms of sale used for assessment
Packing listSupplier / exporterLists packages, quantity and weights so the cargo can be matched and examined
Airway bill (AWB)Airline or its agentThe contract of carriage and the proof of consignment; the key to the delivery order
Goods declaration (GD)Importer or customs agentThe electronic declaration filed with customs for assessment
Delivery order (D/O)Airline / handling agentAuthorises the release of the cargo from the warehouse after charges are paid
Importer registration detailsImporterIdentifies the person or company making the import

Documents needed in some cases

  • Certificate of origin if a preferential duty rate or an origin check applies.
  • Regulatory approvals or licences for controlled goods such as medicines, chemicals, telecom equipment or food.
  • Material Safety Data Sheet and DG declaration for dangerous goods.
  • Insurance and freight documents where they form part of the declared value.
  • Letter of credit or banking documents when payment is through a bank.

The consistency check that saves days

Before the flight departs, place the invoice, packing list and airway bill side by side and check these points: the consignee name and address are identical, the description of goods agrees, the number of packages matches, the weights are consistent, and the invoice value is the value you intend to declare. Differences between documents are a common reason for queries, and fixing them at origin is far easier than after the cargo lands.

Example: what a delay really costs

Take 80 kg of general cargo. Cleared inside the free window on day 3, the total in our Gerry'sDnata calculator is Rs. 18,958. If a missing certificate delays release to day 5, the total becomes Rs. 24,662, because rent of Rs. 4,960 is added together with extra tax. A document problem that takes two days to fix costs Rs. 5,704 before you pay any agent fee.

Tips for a clean file

  1. Ask the supplier for scanned copies as soon as the cargo is booked.
  2. Use the same company name everywhere, spelled identically.
  3. Keep digital copies in a single folder shared with your agent.
  4. Ask the airline or agent to confirm the arrival and the document set needed for the D/O.
  5. Keep proof of every payment.

When to collect each document

StageDocuments to have ready
Before shipmentProforma invoice, permits, licences, product specifications
At dispatchCommercial invoice, packing list, certificates, airway bill copy
Flight confirmedFull scanned set sent to your agent for the goods declaration
On arrivalOriginal or released AWB, payment proof, importer details for the D/O

If a document is missing

  1. Tell your agent immediately; do not wait for the cargo to arrive.
  2. Ask the supplier to send a corrected scan the same day.
  3. Ask whether customs will accept a temporary alternative or an amendment.
  4. Keep written confirmation of every request, with timestamps.

Frequently asked questions

Can I clear cargo with only a photocopy of the AWB?

Requirements vary by airline and by whether the shipment is prepaid or collect. Ask the handling agent which form they accept for the D/O.

What if the invoice value is wrong?

Correct it with the supplier before shipment. Under-invoicing or inconsistent values can lead to customs queries or penalties.

Who prepares the goods declaration?

Usually your licensed customs agent, using your invoice and packing list.

Do samples need the same documents?

Yes, though values and exemptions may differ. Ask your agent about requirements for samples.

How long should I keep clearance documents?

Keep them for as many years as your tax and customs obligations require. Your accountant can confirm the period.
