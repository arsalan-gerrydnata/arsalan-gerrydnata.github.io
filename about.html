About This Tool

Why we built a free godown rent calculator, and how it works.

Import cargo tariffs are published as dense tables — day brackets, weight bands, and separate rate sheets for general, dangerous goods and cold storage cargo. Working out an accurate total by hand means cross-referencing several tables correctly, every time a shipment's numbers change. Small mistakes are easy to make and can lead to unpleasant surprises at the counter.

This calculator was built to remove that friction. Enter an arrival date, a weight, and a cargo category, and it applies the exact published tariff structure — including free-storage windows, day-bracket rates, and weight-based handling tiers — to produce an instant, itemised breakdown: D/O fee, documentation charges, handling charges, godown rent/demurrage, and sales tax.

Who this is for

  • Importers tracking what an in-transit shipment will cost to clear
  • Customs clearing agents quoting charges to clients
  • Freight forwarders comparing the cost of different clearance timelines
  • Anyone trying to understand why a storage bill came out the way it did

Accuracy

The calculation logic mirrors the published tariff structure exactly, tier by tier. That said, tariffs are revised periodically, and this tool reflects rates effective from a specific date. Always confirm final charges directly with your warehouse operator or clearing agent before making payment decisions — see our Privacy Policy for the full terms of use.

How the calculators work

Every calculator follows the same logic as the printed tariff. First the number of days is counted from arrival to gate-out, with the arrival day counted as day 1. Free days, where the category allows them, are subtracted. The remaining chargeable days are multiplied by the rate for the matching day-bracket and weight tier: a flat daily amount up to the weight cut-off, or a per-kilogram rate above it. Handling is added from the weight band, then the fixed D/O and documentation charges, and finally 15% sales tax is applied to the subtotal.

Because the tariff differs between operators, we keep a separate calculator for each: Gerry'sDnata, SAPS (Shaheen and Thai) and PIA, with a Menzies calculator planned. Each page lists its own categories, fixed charges and optional items such as D/O fee and console charges.

How we keep the figures reliable

  • Rates are entered directly from the published tariff sheets, not estimated.
  • Every calculator is tested against edge cases: weights on each side of a cut-off, the first and last day of every bracket, and the boundary of free days.
  • Where a tariff does not publish a charge, we show it as "to be confirmed" rather than invent a number.
  • The worked examples in our guides are generated from the same logic as the calculators, so they always agree.
