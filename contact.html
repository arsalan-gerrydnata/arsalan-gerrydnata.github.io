What to include

To help us answer quickly, please mention:

  • Which calculator you used (Gerry'sDnata, SAPS or PIA)
  • The cargo category, weight, arrival date and gate-out date
  • The figure you expected and the figure the calculator showed
  • A link to the tariff sheet, if you are reporting a rate that has changed

What we can help with

  • Reporting a calculation that looks wrong
  • Tariff or rate updates for a shed already on the site
  • Suggestions for new calculators or guides
  • General feedback on the site

We usually reply within a few working days. We cannot give legal or customs advice, and we cannot see or change your actual bill. For disputes about a charge, please contact your warehouse operator or clearing agent. See also our FAQ.
